HOW TO PLAY

⚠ BETA RELEASE

This is a BETA release. Items, progress, and account data may be lost or corrupted by future updates.

If you're stuck or something's gone wrong, contact the developer at ianmcgon@gmail.com — but please note: the developer does NOT have access to your login credentials, accounts/profiles cannot be retrieved or edited on your behalf, and lost items cannot be restored.

Belt Runner-class shuttle, docked at Meridian Station. A warp gate nearby leads out to Haven Reach — a safe asteroid field patrolled by the Galactic Navy. Longer-range STAR MAP jumps and AUTOPILOT reach every other zone in the galaxy; physical inter-galaxy gates lead to three more galaxies beyond that.

CONTROLS
Click/Tap a contact — select    Double-click/tap space — approach point
Drag — orbit camera    Scroll / Pinch — zoom
Approach / Orbit / Keep Range work on any selected contact; Lock is only needed to Mine, Engage weapons on, or Salvage from something
Set a THROTTLE CAP (25% / 50% / FULL) on the telemetry panel, or hit STOP next to it to kill your velocity — either way you'll cruise forward even with nothing targeted
Esc (or the on-screen SETTINGS button) opens the settings menu / backs out of inspect mode
KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
T target closest    H target closest hostile    M target closest minable    S target closest salvageable
← / → cycle target by distance (closer / farther)    E engage weapons on locked hostile
1 / 2 / 3 / 4 — throttle 25% / 50% / FULL / STOP
Z star map    C cargo    D data pad    R reload (equip most valuable owned ammo)
Shift+H show/hide the whole HUD
Shortcuts are ignored while typing in chat or any other text field
DOCKING & STATIONS
DOCK once in range of a station to open its docking bay menu — UNDOCK launches you back out near the local gate
While docked: REPAIR HULL, sell cargo/ore, browse the MARKET, list/browse the AUCTION HOUSE, equip/unequip owned gear by clicking it in STORAGE, buy/finance ships at the FINANCE OFFICE, take a LOAN, queue MANUFACTURING jobs, craft at the LIBRARY, check your DATA PAD, and take contracts from the GALACTIC NAVY, MINING CORP and TRANSPORTATION GUILD offices
Every station buys/sells at its own market prices and collects its own small STATION TAX on market trades, auction fees and Library crafting — funding the SECTOR GOVERNMENT panel's tax trust for that station
STORAGE and crafting output are per-station — items you bank or craft at one station stay there until you fly back or move them yourself
MINING
Equip a Mining Laser by clicking it in the STORAGE panel's cargo column, lock an asteroid, then hit MINE to extract iron/copper/silver/gold ore into your cargo hold
Sell ore at any station's MARKET, or list it on the AUCTION HOUSE — richer ore types are worth more but rarer
The Mining Corp office pays contracts for ore turn-ins and builds your reputation there
COMBAT
Engage Weapons on a locked hostile — your to-hit % grows slowly as you level combat skills
Shields regenerate over time once you're clear of fire for a few seconds — hostiles carry none, only hull. Once your own shields collapse, hits start damaging your hull for real
Kills leave wreckage to SALVAGE for scrap + a black box (or, rarely, a captain's black box) — turn these in to the Galactic Navy office, or sell/auction them like any other stored item
Warning: a destroyed hull drops you into a weak, unarmed escape pod at the wreck site — board a stored ship or buy a new one at the FINANCE OFFICE to get back in the fight
WARP & AUTOPILOT
Fly up to a physical warp gate and hit WARP for a short local jump — a brief alignment + hyperspace cinematic plays before you arrive
The STAR MAP panel charges up a slower, longer-range direct warp to any zone in your current galaxy from anywhere — the countdown shows at the top-middle of the screen and can be cancelled any time before it fires
The UNIVERSE MAP shows every galaxy at once — click a sector to select/zoom in on it, then press WARP (in range) or AUTOPILOT (further out) to actually go there
AUTOPILOT flies a short countdown, then chains one or more full-charge direct warps toward your destination on its own — it never uses gates, so every leg takes the same full warm-up time a manual STAR MAP warp would. Any manual input cancels it instantly
Autopilot and long-range star-map warps only travel within your current galaxy — crossing into another galaxy needs an inter-galaxy gate flown manually
SKILLS, LIBRARY & KNOWLEDGE
Skills train automatically over real wall-clock time from the SKILLS panel — training keeps advancing even while docked or offline
Knowledge (shown in the DATA PAD's LEDGER tab) accrues from active playtime and from kills/mining/quests/medals — spend it at any station's LIBRARY
The Library crafts Memory Cubes (banked Knowledge you can later burn to instantly speed up whatever skill is training) and Skill Books (scribe once a skill hits LV.1+, to unlock training the next levels) — both cost Knowledge + credits (plus station tax) and take real time to finish, landing in that station's STORAGE when done
AUCTION HOUSE
List anything sitting in a station's STORAGE for other players to buy — set your own price, up to 4 active listings per station
Listings elsewhere show up under "my listings" until sold or cancelled; sale proceeds (minus station tax) land in your DATA PAD automatically
DATA PAD & MAIL
The DATA PAD tracks your LEDGER (credit + Knowledge history), lifetime STATS, earned MEDALS (fixed achievements plus infinitely-scaling tiered chains), your EQUIPMENT, any active LOANS, and your MAIL
Type in the event log to broadcast a message to other players sharing your zone
SHIPS & FINANCING
Ships are items now: an owned-but-not-flown ship sits in a station's STORAGE — click it there (or at the FINANCE OFFICE) to board/equip it, swapping out whatever you're currently flying (which becomes a stored item there in turn)
The FINANCE OFFICE finances ships and starter gear (and now also handles paying down any active loan, directly in this same panel) — financed items are delivered straight to that station's STORAGE, and a loan balance builds against you (see the DATA PAD's LOANS tab). Only one financed SHIP loan, and separately only one financed weapon/shield/ammo GEAR loan, can be open at a time
Payments are due every in-game WEEK. Miss enough and a bounty hunter is sent after you — if one kills you, your current Knowledge balance is wiped on top of the usual death penalty
A brand-new character starts with no ship at all, just an escape pod — finance your first hull at any FINANCE OFFICE

WAYFARER-CLASS

Balanced combat hull: carries weapons and shields, but no mining laser mount. Faster and more agile than the Prospector, with a lighter hull and smaller cargo hold. The pick for fighting, bounty hunting, and general-purpose running.

PROSPECTOR-CLASS

Dedicated mining hull: carries a mining laser and shields, but no weapon hardpoints at all — it can't fight. Tougher hull and nearly double the cargo hold of the Wayfarer, but noticeably slower to accelerate and a lower top speed. The pick for hauling ore out of the belt.
