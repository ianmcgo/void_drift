HOW TO PLAY

Belt Runner-class shuttle, docked at Meridian Station. A warp gate nearby leads out to Haven Reach — a safe asteroid field patrolled by the Galactic Navy. Longer-range STAR MAP jumps reach two more zones: the mildly dangerous Kestrel Belt and the deadly Maelstrom, plus wherever the green-zone warp gates physically connect.

Click/Tap a contact — select    Double-click/tap space — approach point
Drag — orbit camera    Scroll / Pinch — zoom
Lock target, then Approach / Orbit / Keep Range
Engage Weapons on locked raiders — your to-hit % grows slowly as you level up
Shields regenerate over time (after a few seconds free of fire) — raiders carry none, only hull. Once your own shields collapse, hits start damaging your hull for real
Kills leave wreckage to salvage for scrap + a black box. Mine asteroids with a mining laser for ore.
Dock at a station to repair hull damage, visit the MARKET/FITTING to buy and equip gear, and take on jobs at the GALACTIC NAVY and MINING CORP offices for credits and reputation.
Buy or finance ships at the SHIPYARD — new ships go to storage, where you can board, swap or sell them. The Prospector-class mining ship can mount a mining laser but no weapons; the starter ship is the reverse.
Warning: a destroyed hull loses your current XP progress (never a level) and drops you into a weak, unarmed escape pod at the wreck site — board a stored ship or buy a new one at the SHIPYARD to get back in the fight
Track reputation, achievements and your ledger from the WALLET popup    type in the event log to broadcast a message
